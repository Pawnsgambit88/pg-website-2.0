CANONICAL STATES

The interface should never make people guess.

These are production states, not developer placeholders.

LOADING

Keeping your place.

Short, specific language tied to the task. Never “Loading…” as the whole experience.

EMPTY

No games yet.

Explain why and offer the next useful action.

PAYMENT PENDING

Your place is held for 10 minutes.

Show exact expiry and never mark paid until the server verifies.

WAITLIST

No payment taken.

Position, what happens next, and how PG will contact the player.

PAIRING PENDING

You’re checked in. We’re finding the game.

No refresh required. Surface the pairing when published.

ERROR

We couldn’t complete that.

Say what remains safe and the smallest recovery action.

PERMISSION

This action is restricted.

Never expose a hidden admin capability just because the UI can render it.

SUCCESS

You’re playing.

Confirm what happened and what the user needs next.
