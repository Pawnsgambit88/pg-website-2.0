# Content Guide

## Launch editorial baseline

The canonical launch copy is documented in `COPY-BASELINE.md`. Do not rewrite it during implementation for SEO, UI convenience, or personal preference. The CMS may change it after launch, but the repository baseline must render the approved voice exactly.


## Voice
Warm, dry, confident, specific. PG can be witty but must never sound like it is trying hard to be a chess brand.

## Prefer
- “Find my next game”
- “Come solo. We'll make the first hello easy.”
- “Social chess, done properly.”
- “A chess club people actually come back to.”
- “Save my place”
- “What the regulars say”
- “Upcoming games”

## Avoid
- “vibrant community”
- “passionate chess enthusiasts”
- “where strategy meets fun”
- “checkmate boredom”
- “unlock your potential”
- invented claims, event counts or testimonials

## Event copy
Lead with the actual experience. Then disclose format, time control, price and logistics. Do not make people decode chess administration before deciding whether they want the evening.

## Testimonials
Exact approved quote. Never rewrite a member quote to fit layout. Shorten only with explicit editorial approval and preserve meaning.
